Privacy Policy — What If Studio

What If Studio is a personal desktop tool used by a single creator to upload their own animated videos to their own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name) — to confirm which account is connected.
  • The ability to upload videos as drafts to the connected account (video.upload).
  • Public statistics of the creator's own videos (video.list) — to analyze performance.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only locally on the creator's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

Deletion

Disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.
